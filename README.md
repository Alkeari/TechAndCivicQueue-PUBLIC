# Tech and Civic Queue

Hold Shift and click Technologies and Civics to queue several research targets in order, each with its prerequisites, including Masteries. The game works through the whole queue without asking you again.

> **Civilization VII, any build. It does not mark saves as depending on it, so you can remove it at any time.**

## Availability

- [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3803576318)

## What It Does

- **Queue as many targets as you like** - click a Technology or Civic as usual, then hold Shift and click more. Each one joins the end of the queue along with whatever prerequisites it still needs.
- **Masteries queue too** - Shift-click the II part of a card to queue its Mastery, whether or not the Technology or Civic itself is researched yet.
- **See the whole plan** - every step of the queue is numbered in research order across the tree, and a queued Mastery is highlighted and numbered on its own part of the card.
- **Change your mind in one click** - Shift-click anything already queued to take it out, and the numbers close up behind it.
- **No prompts in between** - when one target finishes, research moves straight on to the next without asking you to choose.
- **Two separate queues** - the tech tree and the civic tree each keep their own queue, and both are kept in your save.
- **Knows when it is out of date** - a short notice at the main menu says when a newer version is out, and stays silent otherwise.
- **The base game's own click is unchanged** - a plain click still sets a single target and replaces the queue, and picking a research from the pop-up chooser clears it.

## Requirements

- Civilization VII on any build. The mod declares no minimum game version.
- The base game's `base-standard` module, which every install already has.
- A mouse and keyboard. Queueing is a Shift-click, so a controller cannot add to the queue.
- Verified on game version 1.5.0.

## Installation

1. Start or load a game, open the Tech tree, click a Technology, then hold Shift and click another. The numbers carry on from the first target to the second.

## Configuration

The mod is on whenever it is enabled under Additional Content and off when it is not. Its one setting is under **Options**, **Add-Ons**, **Tech and Civic Queue**:

- **Check for Updates** (on by default) - once a day, at the main menu, the mod reads [alkeari.com's update list](https://alkeari.com/updates/index.json) and shows a short notice only when a newer version is out.
- Nothing else is sent, and nothing is shown when you are up to date. Turn the setting off to skip the check.

## Compatibility

- **Saves stay yours.** The queue is stored in the save, but the save does not depend on the mod. Load it without the mod and research carries on toward the game's own single target.
- **It hooks rather than replaces.** It adds to the game's own tech and civic trees instead of replacing their screens, so another mod that changes those trees still loads alongside it.
- **No game data changes.** It writes nothing to the game's tables, and no gameplay value changes with it.
- **Not with Shift Que.** That mod does the same job, Shift-click queueing on both trees, so enable one or the other.
