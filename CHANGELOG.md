# Changelog

What changed in Tech and Civic Queue, newest first. Only changes a player can notice are listed.
This file is the source of the change notes published to the Steam Workshop and Nexus Mods.

## v1.1.0 - 2026-10-19

- Added: A short notice at the main menu says when a newer version of Tech and Civic Queue is out, at most once a day, with a Check for Updates setting under Options, Add-Ons to turn it off.
- Fixed: A Mastery being researched stays first in the queue when more Technologies or Civics are Shift-clicked after it, instead of being dropped.
- Fixed: A Mastery removed from the queue loses its queued highlight at once.

## v1.0.0 - 2026-09-18

- Added: Shift-click a Technology or Civic in its tree to add it to a queue, researched after the targets already queued along with its own prerequisites.
- Added: Shift-click a queued Technology or Civic again to remove it from the queue.
- Added: Shift-click the Mastery level of a Technology or Civic to queue it, before or after the Technology or Civic itself is researched.
- Added: A queued Mastery level is highlighted and numbered on its own part of the card.
- Added: The tree numbers every step of the whole queue in research order, and the queue is kept in the save.
